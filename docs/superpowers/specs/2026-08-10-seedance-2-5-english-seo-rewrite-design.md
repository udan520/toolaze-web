# Seedance 2.5 English SEO Rewrite Design

## Goal

Rewrite only the English Seedance 2.5 model landing-page content around a verified version thesis while keeping the live Toolaze generator and all non-English locales unchanged.

## Approved content direction

The page will target the model-name generator intent with the thesis that Seedance 2.5 expands Seedance 2.0 in three concrete ways:

1. Continuous generation increases from 4–15 seconds to as much as 30 seconds.
2. Multimodal direction expands from 9 images, 3 videos, and 3 audio clips to as many as 50 reference inputs.
3. The model direction moves toward reference-to-video motion control and more precise localized editing for longer, reference-heavy production briefs.

The first two deltas can appear as direct comparisons. The third must be presented as the official product direction, not as a benchmarked quality claim.

## Evidence boundary

- Seedance 2.0 facts come from ByteDance Seed's official launch page and the Seedance 2.0 technical report.
- Seedance 2.5 facts come from Dreamina's official Seedance 2.5 page.
- Toolaze-specific controls come only from the live generator contract: 4–30 seconds, 480p/720p, up to 30 images, 10 videos, and 10 audio clips, first/last-frame mode separated from multimodal mode, and MP4/MOV output.
- The copy must not present 4K, 180-second beta generation, or localized editing as controls currently available in Toolaze.
- The copy must not claim that Seedance 2.5 introduced multimodal input or native audio because Seedance 2.0 already had both.

## Page structure

1. Hero and live generator
2. What Seedance 2.5 changes
3. Key Features of Seedance 2.5
4. Seedance 2.5 vs Seedance 2.0
5. How to use the Toolaze generator
6. Four model-relevant use cases
7. Toolaze generation settings
8. FAQ

The duplicate `intro` and `competitorComparison` surfaces will be removed from `sectionsOrder`. `performanceMetrics` remains only as a compact Toolaze settings reference immediately before FAQ.

## Content constraints

- Keep the exact primary intent `Seedance 2.5 AI Video Generator` in title and H1.
- Explain practical outcomes instead of using unqualified phrases such as “better quality,” “more stable,” or “production-ready.”
- Use exactly four use cases.
- Do not add prompt-example cards until matching result media exists.
- Keep public English content and the existing SEO Factory English artifact byte-for-byte equivalent as parsed JSON.
- Do not edit other locales, generator behavior, navigation, sitemap, route, or media assets.

# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Creators, marketers, ecommerce teams, and filmmakers evaluating whether Seedance 2.5 fits a reference-led AI video workflow. They need to see real outputs, understand the model's practical limits, and move from a creative brief to a generated video without learning a separate production system.

## Product Purpose

Toolaze gives users direct access to current AI generation models through focused landing pages with a working generator. The Seedance 2.5 page should help users decide when the model is useful, understand how Toolaze exposes its controls, and start a real generation with accurate expectations.

## Brand Personality

Proof-led, cinematic, precise.

## Anti-references

- Generic identical feature-card grids with repeated icons.
- Abstract AI artwork that does not prove the model output.
- Inflated capability claims that are unavailable in the Toolaze generator.
- Dense marketplace pages that bury the working generator under unrelated navigation, pricing, and testimonial content.
- Mock interfaces that differ from the actual Toolaze workflow.

## Design Principles

1. Show the result before explaining the feature.
2. Connect every capability to a visible workflow or decision.
3. Distinguish official model direction from controls currently available in Toolaze.
4. Prefer a few strong media proofs over repeated decorative cards.
5. Keep the working generator as the primary conversion path.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA for contrast, keyboard access, semantic headings, media controls, reduced-motion behavior, and descriptive alternative text. Do not rely on color, animation, or text embedded in screenshots as the only source of meaning.
